How to Wake When Laptop is Sleeping
  1. Enable Wake on Wi-Fi in Windows:
    Open Device Manager → Network Adapters → Your Wi-Fi → Properties → Power Management tab → Check "Allow this device to wake the computer".
  2. When lid is closed:
    In Windows Search, open "Change what closing the lid does" → Set "When I close the lid" to Do Nothing.
  3. Waking from your phone:
    Download any free Wake-on-LAN app (e.g. "Wolow" on iOS or "Wake On Lan" by Aquila on Android) and enter the MAC Address shown above. One tap will wake your PC from sleep!
